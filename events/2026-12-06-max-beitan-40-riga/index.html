The evening

Max Beitan marks his 40th birthday on stage, with friends and colleagues around him. Across the evening the cello is the voice that connects Baroque music, virtuoso classics, operatic bel canto, Argentine tango and jazz improvisation.

The concert takes place in the Grand Ballroom of the Grand Hotel Kempinski Riga and is presented by Beitan Music Foundation.

Programme highlights

  • Antonio VivaldiConcerto for Two Cellos, with string ensemble; with Nikolajs Šugajevs
  • Isaac AlbénizAsturias (Leyenda), arranged for cello
  • Dmitri ShostakovichWaltz No. 2
  • Ennio MorriconeGabriel’s Oboe from The Mission
  • Georges Bizet & Jules MassenetOpera themes, including Carmen
  • Astor PiazzollaLibertango and Argentine tango
  • Juan Tizol & Duke EllingtonCaravan; jazz dialogues for cello, saxophone and piano

These are the highlights of the programme, not the final running order. Individual works, arrangements and performers may still be refined.
